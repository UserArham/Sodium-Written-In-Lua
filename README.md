## Optimization

This Lua mod is **key to optimization**. It attempts to give you a polished interface, smooth and accurate movements, and is trying to get a smoother feel in your client. If you wished to have 100+ FPS, you can turn off VSync. 

<details><summary>Notice 📢</summary>
<p>

To achieve a smooth interface, turn on VSync. If you care about higher frames, turn off VSync, though this may cause screen tearing. If your screen starts to get laggy while VSync is off, turn VSync on. 

</p>
